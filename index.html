Privacy policy

Wizard Apprentice for Android
Package name: gr.fantasia.wizapp
Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy covers the Android game Wizard Apprentice, published by Bill Kotsias (Fantasia). It is a puzzle game for ages 6 and up, and for adults.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or share personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services. It does not use the internet.

Information stored on the device

The player name you type, and that player's progress, are saved only in the app's private storage on the phone. That information stays on the device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's storage in Android settings, deletes it.

Children

The game is suitable for children from the age of 6. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Nothing the child enters is sent off the device.
